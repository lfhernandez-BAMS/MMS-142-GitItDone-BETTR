Privacy Policy

At BETTR, your privacy matters. Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you browse our website, contact us, or place an order. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

Introduction

This website is owned and operated by BETTR. We respect your privacy and understand the importance of knowing how your personal information is collected, used, and protected. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information that you provide when visiting our website, contacting us, or submitting an order inquiry.

In this policy, “you” and “your” refer to visitors and users of the BETTR website. By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you have any questions or concerns about how your personal information is handled, you may contact us through the contact information provided on our website.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, or data-handling practices. Any revisions will be published on this page, along with the updated date. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how their personal information is handled.

01. Your Information

BETTR may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us or placing an order, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your selected product, size, and quantity
  • Any additional information you include in your message or order inquiry

Our website may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type or website usage data, if analytics or similar technologies are implemented.

02. Information Security

The information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and product inquiries
  • Receive, review, and process order requests
  • Communicate with you regarding your order
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website and customer experience, where applicable

BETTR will only use personal information for legitimate purposes related to your interaction with the store.

03. Email Communications

BETTR uses an email-based ordering process. When you click the Order Now button, your email application may open a pre-filled message containing your selected product, size, quantity, and other relevant order details.

You can review and edit the message before sending it. The information you choose to send will be received through the store's designated email account and used to respond to your order request.

Please note that your email provider may process your information according to its own privacy policy.

04. Information Sharing

BETTR does not sell your personal information. Your information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website or respond to your requests, or when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

Any sharing of personal information should be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

05. Data Retention and Security

BETTR will take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve relevant disputes.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request the deletion or blocking of information when legally appropriate
  • Object to certain forms of processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise concerns about how your personal information is handled

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, please contact BETTR using the contact information provided on our website.

07. Cookies and Third-Party Services

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if necessary for its functionality or if third-party services, such as analytics tools, are implemented.

If these technologies are used, BETTR should provide appropriate information about their purposes and any relevant choices available to visitors.

08. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BETTR may update this Privacy Policy when its website, services, or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page, together with the revised date.

09. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, you may contact us through the contact details provided on the BETTR website.
